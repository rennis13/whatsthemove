What’s The Move: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026
Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Who we are

What’s The Move (“we”, “us”) is a mobile app that lets people share where they plan to go with their friends. The app is run by Ryan Ennis, 159 M Street, Boston, MA 02127, USA. Questions about this policy: ryanennis13@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Phone number. Used to create your account and sign you in with a text message code.
  • Profile. Your name, username, and an optional profile photo.
  • Moves. The place you choose, its map coordinates, your arrival time, and an optional note (up to 30 characters).
  • Friend activity. Friend requests, your friends, people you block, and moves you join. Parties you create (title, description, place, date and time, and the friends you invite) and your replies to party invitations (going, maybe, or can’t go).
  • Reports. If you report someone, we save who you reported, the reason you chose, and when.
  • Messages to us. Anything you send to our support email.

Information from your device, only with your permission

  • Contacts. If you tap “Find friends from my contacts,” the app reads the phone numbers in your contacts and sends them to our servers to check which belong to What’s The Move users. We do not store these numbers in our database. Contacts access is optional.
  • Photos. The picture you choose for your profile, any photos you add to a party, and a picture for a party you host.
  • Location. If you allow it, your phone uses your location to show your position on your own map and to put nearby places first in search results. We do not store your current location, and it is never shown to other users. Other users only see the future places you choose to share as moves.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical logs kept by our hosting providers (such as IP address, device type, and time of requests) for security and to keep the service running. We do not currently use advertising or analytics trackers.

3. How we use information

We use your information to create and secure your account, show your moves to your friends, help you find friends, run safety features such as blocking and reporting, investigate misuse and enforce our terms, respond to support requests, improve the app, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information. When you invite someone to the app or to a party by text or email, the message is sent from your own phone or email app, and we do not see or store who you invite.

4. What other people can see

  • Your moves are visible only to your accepted friends. There are no public moves. Parties are visible only to the host and the people invited. The host sees each guest’s reply, and guests only see how many people are going. Photos added to a party, and the party’s picture, are visible to the host and everyone invited, through private links that expire. A host can allow chosen guests to invite their own friends and to see the party’s invite code, and the people they invite can see the party and the host’s name and photo. If you join a party with an invite code, the host can see your name, photo, and reply, and you can see the host’s name and photo, even if you are not friends.
  • Your name, username, and photo are visible to your friends. People who have your phone number saved in their contacts may also see your name and photo when they search their contacts, and can send you a friend request. Anyone who types your exact username can also find your name and photo and send you a friend request. Anyone you have a pending request with can see them too.
  • Joining a move. When you join a friend’s move, people who can see that move can see that you joined. If they are not your friend, they only see that someone else joined, not who.
  • Your current location is not visible to anyone.
  • Profile photos are stored so that anyone who has the direct link to a photo can open it. The links are long and not listed anywhere, but you should not use a photo you would not want shared.
  • Blocking. If you block someone, they can no longer see your moves or send you requests, and they are not notified.
  • Close friends and hidden moves. Your close friends list is private, and no one can see who is on it. You can show a move only to your close friends, or hide it from specific friends, and they are not told.

5. Who we share information with

We use service providers to run the app. They handle information only to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, and file storage.
  • Twilio: sends the text message code to your phone number when you sign in.
  • Apple: the map is provided by Apple Maps.
  • Place search: when you search for a place, the text you type and your approximate location are sent to a place search service (currently Photon, run by Komoot, using OpenStreetMap data).
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect people’s safety and our rights. If we are involved in a merger or sale, information may transfer to the new owner, and we will tell you.

We do not share your information with advertisers.

6. Sponsored venue listings

We do not currently show ads or sponsored content. If we introduce sponsored venue listings, we will clearly label them as “Sponsored,” we will not give advertisers your personal information, and we will update this policy before the listings launch.

7. How long we keep information

  • We keep your account information while your account exists.
  • A move stops appearing 12 hours after it starts. We delete past moves automatically 30 days after they started, but we keep parties and their photos as a history until the host deletes the party or the host’s account is deleted. Photos you add are also removed if you delete them or delete your account.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Profile, then Delete account). This deletes your profile, photo, moves, friendships, close friends list, blocks, and any reports you made or that were made about you. Copies in backups may remain for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile or change your photo at any time in the app.
  • Remove friends, block people, and report problems in the app.
  • Turn off contacts, photos, or location access at any time in your iPhone Settings. The app still works without them.
  • Delete your account in the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU, or the UK), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your information, and to object to or limit certain uses. Email ryanennis13@gmail.com and we will respond within the time required by law.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and database rules that limit who can see each move. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

What’s The Move is not for anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under that age has an account, we will delete it.

11. Where information is processed

Our servers are located in the United States (Oregon). If you use the app from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed there.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app or by another method before they take effect, and we will update the date at the top.
